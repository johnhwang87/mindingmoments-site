Support

I missed the swipe pattern

The pattern is shown once on first launch. If you dismissed it, delete and reinstall the app: it keeps no data anywhere else, so a reinstall starts fresh and shows the pattern again. Reinstalling is free; the App Store remembers your purchase.

The swipes don't open anything

Swipe anywhere on the quote screen, one clear swipe per direction, without pausing for more than about two seconds between swipes. Nothing on screen reacts until the final swipe, when the phone vibrates briefly and the hidden side opens.

Reminders aren't arriving

Open the iPhone Settings app, choose Notifications, find MindingMoments and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Then open the app once so it can reschedule. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself and need the app to have been opened at least once in the last few months to keep the queue topped up.

Moving to a new phone

Because nothing is stored outside your phone, your dates don't transfer automatically. An iCloud or iTunes backup of the whole phone does carry them across; otherwise, enter your dates again on the new device.

Deleting my data

Delete the app. All of its data goes with it.
