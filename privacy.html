Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

MindingMoments is an iPhone app made by an independent developer (“we”). The short version: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

1. What the app stores

Any dates and settings you enter are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, synced, or shared with us or anyone else. We have no server and no database.

2. What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking code, and no third-party services.

3. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone from the information you enter. They do not pass through any server. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

4. Health data

The app can be used to track a menstrual cycle. We treat this as sensitive. Because it never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and nobody else can obtain it from us. The app does not read from or write to Apple Health.

5. Purchases

The app is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we receive no personal details about you from that purchase.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data. There is nothing to request from us because we hold nothing.

7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will move and the new version will be posted at this address.
