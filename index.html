MindingMoments

Mindful quotes on the surface. A private cycle tracker underneath.

SupportPrivacy Policy

MindingMoments is an iPhone app that looks like a simple motivational quote app, and is one. Behind a secret swipe pattern is a period tracker for people who would rather not have one on their home screen. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no cloud, no analytics.

The swipe pattern is shown once, the first time you open the app.
